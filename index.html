01

Operation

One deliberate route

  1. A

    Authorize

    The account owner signs in on Twitch and Google and explicitly grants access.

  2. B

    Discover

    The relay checks the authorized broadcaster for new public clips and skips known clip IDs.

  3. C

    Transfer

    A temporary copy is downloaded, uploaded to the authorized YouTube channel, then removed.

  4. D

    Remember

    Small operational records prevent intentional duplicate uploads and support safe retries.

02

Guardrails

What this relay will not do

×

No password collection

Authentication happens only on official Twitch and Google consent pages.

×

No arbitrary channels

Only an account owner or authorized manager can connect a channel.

×

No data sales

No advertising profiles, tracking pixels, audience resale, or unrelated data use.

×

No deletion access

The Google permission is limited to uploading videos; it does not grant video deletion access.

Plain-language disclosure

Your authorization remains yours.

Connected accounts can revoke access through their Twitch Connections or Google Account security settings. The relay uses Twitch, YouTube, GitHub Actions, and Upstash only to perform the transfer and retain minimal operational state.
